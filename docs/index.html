New in 1.5

Mods & assets from Discord, plus the new Assets and Packs pages

  • Mods & assets from DiscordPaste a #mods or #assets post link and it finds the files in that post through your open Discord app. No bot, no login.
  • AssetsBrowse every image, Lua and other asset with previews. Filter by folder, add new ones, delete what you don't want.
  • PacksEvery install is one pack you can turn off, uninstall (your original files come back) or update from its Discord post.
  • ProfilesSave your whole setup, mods, packs and their order, and switch in one click.
  • Multi-file installsPick several files or Discord attachments and install them as one pack.
  • Right-click menuTurn a mod on or off, edit it, open its folder or delete it to the Recycle Bin.

For players

Mods, Troubleshoot, Play history, Settings, Logs, Health check

  • Mod managerTurn mods on and off, search, save presets, undo any change.
  • Add mods and packsDrop in .gsc files, assets or a whole .zip pack, safety-checked first.
  • Check before you playEvery mod is compiled with Kronos's own compiler before the game opens.
  • Safe modeAll mods off in one click, back on when you're done.
  • Find the crashing modA guided search that narrows it down for you.
  • Crash suspects and play historyTime played, crashes, and which mods were on when the game crashed.
  • Settings editorKronos's gameplay options, your name and keys, with backups.
  • LogsKronos's logs and crash reports, with errors highlighted.
  • Health checkKronos set up right, Steam folder clean, BO7 update warnings, mod conflicts.

For modders

Turn on Developer mode in Settings

  • StudioStart a new mod from a template in seconds.
  • Code editorGSC highlighting, autocomplete, auto-indent and automatic backups.
  • Live problem checkingCatches loops with no wait, a missing #using and more as you type.
  • Compile checkUses Kronos's own compiler. Errors jump to the line.
  • Test alonePlay with only your mod, then your setup comes back by itself.
  • ManifestsVersion, author and requirements, with missing ones flagged.
  • Live consoleLauncher log, crashes and match results as they happen.

Get started

  1. Set up Kronos and run its launcher at least once.
  2. Download the zip, unzip the whole Kronos Plus folder and run Kronos Plus.exe.
  3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed. Click More info → Run anyway.
